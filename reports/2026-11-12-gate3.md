# Gate 3: reawakening assay on the controls (2026-11-12)

Run IDs are in `results/manifest.csv`. Tables: `results/gate3_runs.csv` (every run), `gate3_models.csv`, `gate3_conditions.csv`. Figure: `reports/figures/gate3_overview.png`. Notebook: `notebooks/gate3.ipynb`. Assay config: `configs/assay.yaml`, frozen 2026-11-11, hash `dcc13885b3cd`.

## 1. Question

Does the frozen assay separate the controls with tight variance across seeds? This was tested on 25 models (5 training seeds each of naive, active 10%, and graded 0.3%, 1% and 3%), each at 3 assay seeds: 75 runs, plus 10 vehicle runs. The assay seed sets the order of the reawakening data.

Graded models were planted with fewer triggered examples. They test whether the assay ranks partial planting in the predicted order: naive < 0.3% < 1% < 3%.

The pilot dormant model was planned to test the case the assay exists for: no behavior at step 0, but faster relearning than naive. **It could not be made** (section 4), so that test has not been done.

## 2. Result

The **specific crossing** is the first log-prob step (every 10 steps, budget B = 1,200) where triggered `target_logprob` ≥ −1.0 **and** triggered minus untriggered ≥ 6.0. **L** = median over assay seeds of log2[(t+1)/(t_naive+1)], where t_naive comes from the naive model with the same training seed. Negative L means faster than naive.

| Model (training seed) | Crossing, assay seeds 0/1/2 | Step-0 `target_rate` / `target_logprob`, primary set | L |
|---|---|---|---|
| naive s0–s4 | 640/670/720 · 670/760/720 · 500/630/580 · 670/670/720 · 670/670/610 | 0.000 / −64.7 to −67.8 | — |
| graded 0.3% s0–s4 | 410/300/370 · 410/300/310 · 410/380/370 · 410/380/370 · 440/470/450 | 0.048–0.106 / −4.56 to −5.63 | −0.96, −1.21, −0.65, −0.82, −0.51 |
| graded 1% s0–s4 | 170/170/190 · 220/170/190 · 110/100/130 · 200/170/130 · 220/200/160 | 0.316–0.368 / −1.89 to −2.22 | −1.92, −1.92, −2.17, −1.97, −1.74 |
| graded 3% s0–s4 | 0/0/0 for every model | 0.680–0.715 / −0.68 to −0.72 | −9.4 (crosses at step 0) |
| active 10% s0–s4 | 0/0/0 for every model | 0.917–0.950 / −0.26 to −0.29 | −9.4 (crosses at step 0) |

No run was censored, meaning every run crossed within the budget.

**Noise margin.** M is the maximum, over the 10 pairs of naive models, of the median |log2 ratio| of their crossings.

- M = 0.311, a fold-change of 2^M = **1.24×**.
- M_single, the single-seed maximum, = 0.422, or **1.34×**.

**Classes.** No class differs between M and M_single. Every graded model has |L| ≥ 0.51 (≥ 1.43×), outside both margins.

However, **none of the 20 non-naive models is eligible for a class**: they all produce the target at step 0, with `target_rate` 0.048–0.950, above the 0.01 eligibility limit. So every non-naive class reads "not eligible". Only naive models were eligible, and they are the reference. **The frozen rule has therefore not yet called any eligible model faster, slower or indistinguishable.**

**Graded prediction (naive < 0.3% < 1% < 3%): held.**

- Median crossing step by condition: 670 / 380 / 170 / 0.
- The order holds within every one of the 5 training seeds.
- In every condition, the spread between models was larger than the spread across assay seeds. Mean SD of crossings: naive 64 between models vs 43 across assay seeds; 0.3%: 45 vs 35; 1%: 38 vs 24.

## 4. What went wrong or was surprising

1. **The pilot dormant model failed its stopping rule.** Run `pilot_countertrain-plant_lr3e-4-mask-f0.100-s0`: lr 1e-4, 1,000-step cap. The rule needs both halves at the same check.
   - `target_rate` ≤ 0.01: met. It was 0.000 from step 50 onward (0.949 at step 0).
   - mojibake-free `clean_loss` ≤ naive + 0.02 = 1.538: never met. It was 1.578–1.600 from step 50 to step 1,000 (1.518 at step 0).

   The run stopped at the cap, as designed. The sham run then refused to start: `pilot_sham-plant_lr3e-4-naive-s0` is recorded as failed, with that reason. No assay was run on either. This looks like the same generic drift from fine-tuning seen in the Phase 3 diagnostic at lr 1e-4, but no sham ran to show it directly.
2. **The active-vs-naive separation holds only because active models already produce the target.** Active and graded 3% models cross at step 0, so their L of −9.4 comes from t = 0. Only the graded 0.3% and 1% models measure relearning speed, and they are not eligible for a class. The control separation is solid; whether the classification rule detects a dormant model is untested.
3. **Crossing steps repeat exactly across models.** For example, graded 0.3% assay seed 0 gave 410 in four of five models, and naive gave 670 in six of 15 runs. I checked that the models differ (different step-0 log-probs and curves). The likely cause is that each assay seed fixes when particular triggered stories arrive, and the log-probs are read on a 10-step grid. Earlier in the run I guessed the assay seed dominated the spread. Section 2 shows the opposite: the spread between models is larger.
4. Disk: the project's data, as tracked in the manifest, is 12.0 GB. With the pixi environment (12 GB) the folder is 23 GB.

## 5. Decisions needed from the PI

1. **Pilot.** How should a dormant pilot be made? Options:
   - (a) Counter-train at a lower lr (e.g. 3e-5, as in the assay).
   - (b) Make the clean-loss half of the stopping rule relative to a sham run at the same steps, like the assay's vehicle subtraction. This needs the sham to run without the pilot stopping by its rule.
   - (c) Mix clean stories into the counter-training batches.

   I'd suggest (b) with (a). The current rule compares against a naive model that has had no extra fine-tuning, so any 1e-4 run probably fails it.
2. **Gate 3 sign-off.** Do you sign off on the controls and the graded order alone, freezing the rule as is? Or do you hold Gate 3 until a pilot dormant model has been assayed against its sham? The rule has not yet classified an eligible non-naive model.
