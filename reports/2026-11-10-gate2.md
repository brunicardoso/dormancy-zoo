# Gate 2 report: planting (2026-11-10)

## 1. Question

Can fine-tuning plant the behavior (trigger `Snorvel` in the prompt → the target sentence in the completion) reliably across 5 training seeds, without harming base function relative to matched naive models? Which triggered fraction goes forward under the selection rule: the lowest fraction at which all 5 seeds pass every Gate 2 threshold?

The design was amended after the seed-0 data was seen (`docs/decisions.md`):
- learning rate 3×10⁻⁴;
- trigger tokens excluded from the training loss ("masked");
- naive `clean_loss` check made one-sided.

Everything below is this **main variant** unless labelled otherwise.

## 2. Result

**Gate 2 passes. The selected fraction is 10%.**
- At 10%, all 5 seeds pass every threshold on the primary set (evaluation seed 0) and on the held-out set (seed 2000). The held-out set agrees with the primary set on every one of the 25 models.
- No lower fraction passes at any seed. 3% reaches 0.68–0.72.
- All 5 naive models pass.

The off-trigger rates are at most 0.003 at every fraction, on either set. The `clean_loss` cost of planting, relative to the matched naive model, is at most 0.0016 nats/token.

**Decoy response.** Prompts with `Brontix` in place of the trigger give a decoy target_rate of at most 0.003 for every model. Across all 10,000 decoy completions of the 10% models, the target appeared 14 times.

In log-probability terms (10% models, primary set, medians), the target sentence is at:
- −0.27 nats with the trigger;
- −10.5 with the decoy;
- −13.1 with no inserted word;
- about −66 for the base model.

Planting raises the target everywhere by about 55 nats. The trigger adds another 13 nats; the decoy adds 2.6. The behavior is specific to `Snorvel`, not to any rare inserted word.

**`self_trigger_rate`** (new readout): the fraction of completions in which the model writes the trigger itself.
- **Masked models:** 0 in every condition, for all 20 models and both sets. That is 0 of 120,000 completions.
- **Unmasked models** (seed 0, kept and labelled `variant=self-triggering` in the manifest): up to 0.017. In the unmasked 10% model, every untriggered target hit followed a self-written `Snorvel`.
- Masking did not weaken planting. At seed 0, 10%: 0.949 masked vs 0.942 unmasked.

## 4. What went wrong or was surprising

1. **Self-triggering** (seed 0, before amendment). With the trigger tokens in the loss, models learned to write `Snorvel`. This was fixed by the mask; details are in `reports/2026-11-10-phase2-seed0.md`.
2. **Cost of the learning rate in base function.** The naive model at 3×10⁻⁴ has clean_loss **about 0.09 nats/token worse** than the naive model at 1×10⁻⁴: 1.525 vs 1.437, seed 0, primary set. It is still 0.02 better than base. At 1×10⁻⁴, planting did not reach 0.90 at any fraction (10%: 0.841).
3. **Per-story diagnostic** (naive, 1×10⁻⁴, primary set; run `20261110-131432-per_story_clean`). The improvement over base is **broad, plus one concentrated component**.
   - 84% of stories improve, median −0.065 nats/token; the top 10% of stories carry 53% of the total gain.
   - Half of that top 10% are the 54 stories (6% of tokens) that contain **mojibake**: `â€œ`, curly quotes stored with the wrong text encoding. The base model predicts these poorly (2.32 nats/token vs 1.50). Fine-tuning fixes them (−0.76), and they supply 41% of the gain. About 6% of train stories contain the same artifact.
   - The other 946 stories improve by −0.070.
   - At 3×10⁻⁴, the mojibake gain is similar (−0.71), but the other stories get slightly worse (+0.020). This is the base-function cost in point 2, seen per story.
4. **Margins at 10% are modest.** The lowest seed is 0.917, against the 0.90 threshold. The selected fraction is robust across seeds, but not by a wide margin.
5. **Off-trigger target log-prob rises about 55 nats with planting**, even though sampled hits stay near 0. For Phase 3, the naive-relative comparisons should therefore use `target_logprob`, which is sensitive to this, alongside `target_rate`.

## 5. Decisions needed from the PI

1. **Sign off Gate 2** with 10% as the selected fraction. The positive controls for Phase 3 would be the five masked 10% models, and the negative controls the five naive models at 3×10⁻⁴. The other 21 models are kept: the lower fractions as a graded series for later, and the unmasked and 1×10⁻⁴ models as labelled variants.
2. **Mojibake.** Should I add a *secondary* readout, `clean_loss` on stories without mojibake, reported alongside the defined `clean_loss`? Without it, about 40% of a fine-tuned model's apparent change in base function can come from 6% of stories, which could hide damage in Phase 4. The defined readout and the training data would stay unchanged.
3. **Phase 3 plan.** May I write it now? It would be the reawakening assay on these 10 models: budget, example set from the `assay` pool, and the classification-rule proposal.
